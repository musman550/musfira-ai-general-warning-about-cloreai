## Overview

Clore.AI is a cloud-based GPU hosting service that allows users to rent out their excess computing power to others. This service is particularly valuable during times of high demand, such as during the peak hours of online gaming or video streaming, where users can earn money while using their GPUs. For example, a user who has a powerful GPU might rent out this resource for a few hours during their off-peak times, earning a small amount of money. This way, the user can recoup the cost of self-hosting their GPU without having to use it all the time, thus maximizing both their earnings and the efficiency of their computing resources.
